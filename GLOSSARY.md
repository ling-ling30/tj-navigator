# GLOSSARY: Jakarta Multi-Modal Transit Domain

> Canonical domain vocabulary for TJ-Navigator. When discussing routing, transit networks, or UI representations, use these exact terms.

---

## 1. Commuter & Journey Terms

### Journey
The complete point-to-point travel plan chosen by a commuter from an origin to a destination (e.g. from *Tebet Eco Park* to *Grand Indonesia*).

### Itinerary
One candidate route option generated by the routing engine for a given journey query. An itinerary consists of an ordered sequence of **Legs**. A search typically returns 3–5 itineraries (e.g., *Fastest*, *Fewest Transfers*, *Cheapest*, *Rail Priority*).

### Leg
A single continuous segment of travel within an itinerary using one mode and vehicle/walk segment:
- **Transit Leg**: Boarding a vehicle at a stop/station, riding through intermediate stops, and alighting at a transfer or destination stop.
- **Walking Leg**: Walking on foot between the origin and first stop, between stops at a transfer hub (via skywalk/underpass), or from the last stop to the final destination.

### Transfer
The transition between two consecutive transit legs. May involve changing platforms within the same station, or walking through an inter-modal **Transfer Hub**.

### RecountedJourneyDuration
The end-to-end trip duration adjusted in real-time by integrating live vehicle telemetry and traffic bottleneck delays into both the boarding wait time and the in-vehicle transit legs.

### TrafficDelayOffset
The difference between nominal scheduled transit time and live recounted conditions (e.g. `+7 minutes` caused by Kuningan West bottleneck).

### ActiveJourneyPersistence
The serialization of the user's active trip leg, transfer checklist, and recent corridor traffic multipliers to local storage, surviving mid-tunnel phone locks or browser reloads.

## 2. Transit Network & Infrastructure

### TransitMode
The category of public transit service:
- `BRT`: Transjakarta dedicated-corridor bus rapid transit (Corridors 1–14).
- `NON_BRT`: Transjakarta feeder buses sharing mixed traffic.
- `MIKROTRANS`: JakLingko micro-transit vans (angkot) serving residential neighborhoods.
- `COMMUTER_RAIL`: KRL Commuterline heavy rail operated by KAI Commuter.
- `MRT`: MRT Jakarta metro line (heavy subway/elevated).
- `LRT`: Light rail transit (LRT Jabodebek and LRT Jakarta).
- `WALK`: Pedestrian movement.

### Stop / Halte
A bus stop or BRT station where passengers board or alight buses. BRT stations have fare gates (tap-in / tap-out); non-BRT bus stops are roadside signs or shelters with onboard tap readers.

### Station
A heavy rail, metro, or light rail facility (KRL, MRT, LRT) with physical platforms, turnstiles, and ticket gates.

### TransferHub
A designated interchange area connecting multiple transit modes or lines via physical infrastructure (e.g., *Dukuh Atas TOD*, *CSW-ASEAN Interchange*, *Kebayoran Skywalk*, *Manggarai Central Interchange*).

### DirectionalTransferEdge
An asymmetric pedestrian connection from `fromStopId` to `toStopId` that accounts for differing climb vs. descent times (e.g. ascending 5 levels at CSW vs. descending) and one-way turnstile flows.

### PaidAreaTransfer
A transfer occurring strictly inside the paid gate area (e.g. transferring between Corridor 1 and Corridor 13 at CSW, or between KRL lines at Manggarai). Incurs **0s** gate buffer and **Rp 0** additional fare.

### GateBuffer
Pedestrian friction delay for passing through fare turnstiles:
- `0s`: Inside paid area (`isPaidAreaTransfer: true`).
- `45s`: Single gate (boarding street-level bus or non-BRT feeder).
- `75–90s`: Double gate (tapping out of operator A, e.g. KRL, and tapping into operator B, e.g. MRT).

### PeakPenalty
Additional rush-hour delay buffer applied during morning (06:30–09:00 WIB) and evening (16:30–19:30 WIB) peaks to model stair bottlenecks and platform crowding.

### ShelteredSkywalk & StepFreeTransfer
- `isSheltered`: Weather-protected overhead pedestrian bridge or concourse protecting against rain and heat.
- `isStepFree`: Route accessible via elevator or ramp for luggage, strollers, and wheelchairs.

### OfflineOrientationLayer
A bundled 40 KB GeoJSON underlay of primary Jakarta arterial roads (Sudirman-Thamrin, Gatot Subroto, Rasuna Said) and canals rendered on `#0A0E17` to give spatial orientation when raster map tiles cannot load offline.

## 3. Schedules, Time, & Real-Time Telemetry

### Headway
The scheduled time interval between two consecutive vehicles on the same route (e.g., MRT operates with a 5-minute headway during peak hours; Corridor 1 operates with 2–4 minute headway).

### HeadwayWindow
A departure frequency presentation format (e.g., *"Every 6–10 minutes"*) used instead of deceptive minute-exact timetables for non-rail street transit.

### RealtimeEstimate
Live countdown calculated from real-time vehicle GPS telemetry (e.g., *"Bus TJ-204 arriving in ~3 min (1 stop away)"*).

### LiveSegmentRuntimeEngine
The unified runtime calculation engine that models buses on each corridor as live traffic probes, computing both the boarding arrival countdown and the in-vehicle transit travel time.

### ObservedBusHopTime
The measured duration taken by preceding buses to traverse a directional stop-to-stop hop $(i \to i+1)$ on the corridor.

### BaselineHopTime
The calibrated nominal transit time for a stop-to-stop hop under scheduled or free-flow conditions.

### RollingBlendFilter
The exponential smoothing filter blending $70\%$ live observed traversal time with $30\%$ historical time-of-day baseline:
$$\text{Recounted Hop Time} = 0.7 \cdot \text{Observed} + 0.3 \cdot \text{Baseline}$$

### TrafficMultiplier
The ratio of live segment runtime to baseline runtime, bounded between $0.8\times$ (midnight empty busway) and $3.0\times$ (severe intersection gridlock).

### HalteDwellBuffer
A calibrated $+25\text{ seconds}$ delay added per intermediate stop for passenger boarding and alighting.

### LieFiDetection
Strict 2.5-second fetch timeouts and 15-second telemetry heartbeat watchdogs that detect dead-signal "Lie-Fi" in subway tunnels and trigger offline fallbacks without waiting for OS disconnect events.

### GraphVersionHash
A SHA-256 fingerprint of the compiled GTFS transit graph stored in IndexedDB, checked in the background by a Web Worker to avoid blocking mobile startup.

## 4. Ticketing & Fares

### Fare
The monetary cost in Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) for a leg or journey:
- **Flat Fare**: Fixed amount regardless of distance (e.g., Transjakarta BRT/feeder = Rp 3.500; Mikrotrans = Rp 0 with card tap).
- **Distance Fare**: Progressive pricing based on kilometers traveled (e.g., KRL Commuterline = Rp 3.000 first 25 km + Rp 1.000 / 10 km).
- **Station-to-Station Matrix**: Explicit station-pair price table (e.g., MRT Jakarta Rp 3.000 to Rp 14.000).

### IntegratedFare (JakLingko)
The multi-modal integrated tariff capping the maximum fare across participating modes (Transjakarta, MRT, LRT) within a 3-hour window to Rp 10.000.

## Routing v2 terms

### Mode Group
One of the six choices a traveller can switch on or off: BRT, Bus, Mikrotrans, KRL, MRT, LRT. A line belongs to exactly one mode group. Walking belongs to none and is always allowed.

### Travel Options
The traveller's standing preferences for a search: maximum transfers, walking tolerance, transfer time, and whether premium buses are included.

### Mode Suggestion
A single labelled hint shown when a mode the traveller switched off would give a clearly faster journey. It never replaces the options found with the chosen modes.
